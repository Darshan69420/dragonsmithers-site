What happens to your device

Every job follows the same four stages, and you hear from me at each one.

  1. Received

    Drop it off or I come to you. Tell me what it's doing, and send a photo of any error if you have one.

  2. Diagnosed

    I find the actual cause and send you one firm price. Diagnosis is free.

  3. Approved

    No work starts until you say yes. Most jobs are done in 1 to 3 days.

  4. Ready

    You get it back with a written note on what was wrong and what I changed.
