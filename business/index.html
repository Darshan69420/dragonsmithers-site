How it starts

No long sales process. It's the same ticket flow as a home repair, with a walkthrough first.

  1. Walkthrough

    You tell me how the office runs today: who has accounts, where files live, what's been breaking.

  2. Written plan

    I send a short list of what I'd fix first, the one-time setup price, and the monthly price.

  3. Setup

    Nothing changes until you approve it. Then I fix the urgent items and document how everything is set up.

  4. Monthly

    Ongoing admin and help when something goes wrong, from someone who already knows your setup.
